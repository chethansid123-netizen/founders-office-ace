## 1. The idea in one line

> **"Three days. One bag. Zero planning."**

The film opens on the traveller the deck calls **the Compressed Traveller** (60% of the target: ₹15–30 L income, 26–38, three to five short trips a year, *carries two bags today*). It shows the gap in Mokobara's own range (24 L, then 28 L, then nothing), fills it with the Grand Voyage, then walks through the bag litre by litre. It closes on the deck's value proposition, with a real zip sound: *"The bag you stop thinking about the moment you zip it shut."*

## 2. What the research says

### Mokobara's brand and film language
| Element | What Mokobara does | What the film does with it |
|---|---|---|
| **Positioning** | Luggage as identity, not utility. Design-led, digital-first D2C out of Bengaluru, founded by Sangeet Agrawal and Navin Parwal (ex-Urban Ladder). | It sells the trip ("Anywhere-but-here places") as much as the bag. |
| **Voice** | Warm, playful, slightly quirky. Examples: the "Warm Hugs, Mokobara" sign-off, the CTRL+Z damage policy, and colour names like Money Moves, Cool Beans and So Matcha. | Every line comes from the deck's own voice sheet: *Three days. One bag. Zero planning.* · *It dies at the bottom, so we built the bottom.* · *Handles that forgive a long corridor.* · *Anywhere-but-here places.* The film signs off with **"Warm hugs, Mokobara."** |
| **Visual system** | Black, white and one loud yellow. Lots of white space, lowercase round wordmark, product on clean grey. | Black, Mokobara yellow (#FFD400, sampled from the deck) and warm paper white. Mono spec labels like the deck's. The round wordmark is redrawn as vector. |
| **Film** | Celebrity-led and cinematic. Diljit Dosanjh became brand ambassador in Oct 2024; the Mars parody film leaned on over-the-top humour and the 30-day return policy. Creator collabs include Sufi Motiwala (backpacks) and Sonal Tamang (colour collection). | Keeps the cinematic open (airport departures board, flash cuts, product hero), but adds what their films rarely do: **the spec**. |

### How competitors make their films
| Brand | Their film playbook | The gap Grand Voyage can use |
|---|---|---|
| **Away** | Big cinematic travel stories: *Travel Changed Me* (White Lotus tie-in, shot by Łukasz Żal), *Get Away With More* (spy-film rhythm), influencer films directed like fashion. | Emotion, but almost no product truth. |
| **Bellroy** | Craft and detail close-ups. Pages publish **denier, zip brand and water rating**. | The deck's core point is that no Mokobara page states these three, and Bellroy states all of them. |
| **Nasher Miles** | Colour and creators: *"Why so boring, bro?"* with Sakshi Shivdasani and Ayush Mehra for Flipkart BBD; Rishabh Pant as ambassador; Zepto tie-up. | Loud, youthful, price-led. It doesn't read as premium. |
| **Samsonite / American Tourister / VIP / Safari** | Durability claims and hard-shell drop tests. Capital pointed at hard luggage. | Nobody sells a premium **40 L soft duffel** (six premium brands checked; Bellroy's is ₹21,800 and not retailed in India). |

**The strategic move:** Mokobara's warmth plus Bellroy-level disclosure. The film's middle third (13.5–27 s) is the part no Mokobara film has done. It names **840D ballistic nylon, the 1000D Cordura base, YKK #8 AquaGuard and the 1,500 mm water rating** on screen, and shows exactly **where the 40 litres go**.

## 3. Persuasion techniques, and where each one appears

| # | Technique | Where | Why it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Pattern interrupt: the departures board** | 0–2 s | Split-flap flips (TO GOA · FOR 3 DAYS · BAGS **2**) put the viewer at the airport before a single claim. |
| 2 | **Name the behaviour, not the demographic** | 1.4–4.5 s | *"Three days away. Two bags?"*, with a trolley plus a backpack. That's the deck's target doing what it does today. |
| 3 | **Show the gap in the brand's own range** | 4.5–9 s | 24 L (a weekend), 28 L (a gym bag), then an empty dashed slot: *"Three days had nothing. Until now."* |
| 4 | **Product hero on the brand colour** | 9–13.5 s | Yellow wipe, bag drops in, an outlined **40L** behind it. The tagline lands on three beats. |
| 5 | **Proof by anatomy (Bellroy honesty, Mokobara warmth)** | 13.5–27 s | Six features, one per bar, on a live diagram. The zip opens to the yellow lining; clothes, shoes, a 16-inch laptop and a wet pocket fill a **litre meter that counts to 40 / 40 L**. |
| 6 | **Spec rush on the beat** | 27–31.5 s | Eight cards at one per beat: 840D, vegan leather, neoprene handles, strap, trolley sleeve, 54×31×26 cm (squeezing into the cabin box), 1.90 kg, 3-year warranty. |
| 7 | **Emotional turn** | 31.5–38.25 s | A flash into the travellers, *Anywhere-but-here places*, a destination board flipping GOA → COORG → HAMPI → JAIPUR, then the three colourways by name. |
| 8 | **Price after value** | 38.25 s | **₹9,499** only after 29 seconds of proof. There's no struck-through MRP; an anchor would undercut the honesty. |
| 9 | **Sonic payoff** | 41.6 s | A zip closes across the screen as the value proposition finishes: *"...the moment you zip it shut."* |
| 10 | **Brand sign-off** | 42.75 s | The round wordmark spins in, followed by *Warm hugs, Mokobara · mokobara.com*. |
| 11 | **Only claims the deck supports** | Throughout | The deck's own rule (*"Cut from the draft"*): every number is on its spec sheet. No "900D", no "47,000 trips", no invented reviews. |

## 5. The music

The score is **original and synthesised in code** (`source/score.py`), so there's nothing to license. It's a neo-soul house cue in **D major at 106.67 BPM**, which is exactly 20 bars in 45 seconds, so every cut lands on the beat.
- **Harmony:** 9th and 13th chords (Dmaj9 · Bm9 · Gmaj9 · A13sus) played on an FM electric piano with stereo tremolo, over a lush detuned pad.
- **Hook:** a four-bar melody (F♯–E–F♯–A–B … A–F♯–E–D) on a glassy mallet through a dotted-8th ping-pong delay. It plays at the hero, the spec rush, the lifestyle turn and the price. The final phrase resolves home on D under the logo.
- **Groove:** a sidechained sub bass, swung 16th shakers, claps on 2 and 4, and open hats. The groove drops out for the emotional turn and rebuilds for the price.
- **Mix:** stereo convolution reverb, glue compression, low end summed to mono, presence and air lift, mid/side width. Checked to −13 dBFS RMS, −1 dB peak, L/R correlation 0.63, and under 1 dB lost when summed to mono, so it holds up on phone speakers.
- **Sound design on the picture's timeline:** airport chime, plane pass, split-flap clicks, zip opening and closing, a thud when the bag lands, rain and drops, lock click, and a shutter tick on each spec card.

The film uses the **travelling couple from the deck** as its on-screen talent, a warm-graded full-bleed scene at 31.5 s. That is the deck's own image and the only people footage available here. For a full production:

| Role | Recommendation | Why |

| **Voice** | Optional warm, conversational voice-over in Indian English (script below) | The film is written to work muted first. Add the voice only for YouTube and OTT. |

## 8. What's real and what's mocked up

- **The Grand Voyage is a proposal.** Spec, price (₹9,499), colours, warranty and the January 2027 launch all come from the deck. The film makes no claim beyond them.
- **Product images** are the deck's own renders: the 40 L bag cut out from the range lineup, and detail views cropped from the product grid. The 24 L and 28 L bags are the existing Fuji and Bootcamp.
- **The diagram is illustrative.** The litre split (26/6/3/3/2) is the deck's design specification, not a measurement.
- **The couple image** comes from the deck, and **all music and sound** was made for this film.
- **The Mokobara name and wordmark** belong to Mokobara and appear only because this is a pitch to Mokobara.

## 9. How to edit and re-render

```bash
cd source
python3 build.py        # film.template.html → film.html (injects the round wordmark)
python3 score.py score.wav
FFMPEG=$(python3 -c "import imageio_ffmpeg;print(imageio_ffmpeg.get_ffmpeg_exe())") \
  DUR=45 AUDIO=score.wav node render.js ../mokobara_grand_voyage_45s_9x16.mp4 60 film.html   # needs playwright + chromium
```
- **Change the copy:** it's all in `film.template.html`. The anatomy features live in the `FEATS` array and the spec cards in `RUSH`.
- **Preview:** open `film.html?play` to loop it live, or `film.html?t=20` to jump to one moment.
- **Timing:** everything sits on the 2.25 s bar grid (`bar(k)`). Move a visual beat and move the matching hit in `score.py`.
