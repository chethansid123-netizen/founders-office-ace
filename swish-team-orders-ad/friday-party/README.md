**Deliverable:** `swish_friday_party_45s_9x16.mp4`: 1080×1920, 45.0 s, with spoken dialogue, lip-sync, captions, music and sound design.
**Format:** an animated, illustrated story film. It is not live action (see "How to shoot it for real" at the end for the same story with real actors).

## The story, in one line

It's 12:44 on a Friday. The team lead says *"lunch is on me"*, drops one Swish link in the team's WhatsApp group, and nine people order from their own phones. Ten minutes later three teammates bring one delivery up from the lobby, and the team eats together at about 1 PM.

## What's included, beyond the brief

- **The real product flow:** Team Orders card → create → share to WhatsApp → everyone adds → one checkout, with the option to pay for everyone or split via UPI.
- **Research proof points built into the story:**
  - veg packed separately (a red line for part of the survey sample)
  - name labels on every box
  - one kitchen, one rider, one handover
  - a visible team discount
  - arrives in about 10 minutes
- **Swish brand assets:**
  - the traced wordmark and the sparkle chime at the end
  - green bags, the rider's jacket, helmet and scooter
  - the app UI in Swish's style
- **Captions on every line**, so it works with the sound off.
- **Real-time clock chips** (12:44 → 12:45 link sent → 12:48 order placed → 12:58 → 1:00 PM lunch), so the "ordered at 12:45, eating at 1" timing reads without anyone saying it.

## How it was made

- **Voices:** Kokoro, an open-source offline neural text-to-speech model, with one distinct voice per character. Crowd reactions are several voices layered.
- **Lip-sync:** each character's mouth follows the loudness of their own line, frame by frame, so the lips move with the words.
- **Characters:** nine illustrated people built in code, each with their own skin tone, hairstyle, outfit and accessories (glasses, earrings, beard, blazer). They blink, look down at their phones, cheer, eat and walk.
- **Music:** an original upbeat feel-good cue at 116 BPM that ducks under every spoken line.
- **Sound design:** office room tone and keyboards, phone buzzes when the link lands, a pop for each item added, taps, kitchen clatter, lift ding, footsteps, scooter idle, bag rustle and the logo chime.

**Honest limits:** the voices come from a US/UK English model, so they don't have Indian accents, and the film is illustrated rather than photographic. Both are fixed by shooting it for real.

## How to shoot it for real (live-action version of the same 45 s)

1. **Location:** a real tech-park office in HSR Layout or Koramangala, with glass walls, a daylight skyline and laptops. Shoot around 12:30–1:30 for natural lunch light.
2. **Casting:** nine actors aged 25–33 (five women, four men), styled smart-casual in the Bengaluru startup look. Add one real Swish rider in uniform, with a Swish scooter.
3. **Dialogue:** use the lines above as written; they're timed for 45 s. Let the actors ad-lib the reactions (cheer, laughs, "Yes!").
4. **Inserts:** screen-record the phone shots from a working prototype of the Team Orders flow. Shoot the WhatsApp group on a real test group.
5. **Must-get shots:** the phone buzzing on nine desks at once, the lift doors opening, the rider's handover of three bags, name labels on the boxes, and the team eating together.
6. **Sound:** lav mics on the actors. Keep this film's music timing, or re-score it.
7. **Deliverables:** 9:16 for Reels and Stories and 16:9 for YouTube and LinkedIn, both burned-in captioned and clean.

## Re-render

```bash
python3 voices.py kokoro-v1.0.onnx voices-v1.0.bin   # dialogue.wav + dialogue.json (lip-sync)
python3 mix.py                                        # soundtrack.wav (music ducks under voices)
python3 build.py                                      # film.html
DUR=45 AUDIO=soundtrack.wav node render.js swish_friday_party_45s_9x16.mp4 30 film.html
```

To change a line, edit `script.py`; the story timing lives in `film.template.html` (`SHOTS`, `ADD`).
