# Post 02: Daily emotional check-in

**The line:** Pause this reel on a word. That's how today really feels.
**Why:** A game people replay to land on a word, then answer in the comments: replays and comments are what Instagram rewards, and every answer is a small, safe way of naming a feeling.
**Format:** Reel, 9:16, 19 s, with original music
**For:** Everyone: students, employees, young adults
**Post on:** Tue 10 Nov 2026, 8:30 PM IST

## Files, in upload order
1. Reel.mp4 (video with music)
2. Cover.png (cover image)

## How to upload
1. Tap + → Reel → select Reel.mp4. The music is already in the video.
2. Tap Next → Edit cover → Add from camera roll → choose Cover.png.
3. Keep “Share to Feed” on.
4. Paste the caption and hashtags below. Add the alt text under Advanced settings → Accessibility.
5. Post the first comment below from the YourDOST account and pin it.
6. Reply to comments in the first hour. If a comment suggests someone is in crisis, reply kindly with Tele MANAS 14416 (free, 24/7), send a DM, and flag it to the clinical team.
