Screen by screen

This part walks every screen in the order you will show it: what it is for, how to get there, every element on it with the number it shows and where that number comes from, what to click, and what to say. Default values are the ones the platform opens with: Olay for Brand Match (consideration, ₹18L), Beauty for Vertical Lens, and the live Amazon campaign for Campaigns & ROI.

Before you start: the app remembers

The prototype saves your last brand, vertical, campaign, theme and whether "Tune the model" was open in the browser's local storage. If you rehearsed on Amazon tonight, Brand Match will open on Amazon tomorrow. Before the meeting, open Brand Match and pick the brand you want to lead with. All numbers in this part assume a fresh start on Olay.

EMV (earned media value)

A vanity metric that prices every view and like at an industry rate card. Formula used here: EMV = reported views × ₹1.4 + engagements × ₹28. Example: Amazon = 35,13,707 × ₹1.4 + 93,206 × ₹28 = ₹75.3L, which on ₹22.4L spend claims 3.36×.

"How the engine works" pipeline

Six numbered stages: Ingest (creator APIs with consent, campaign history, Represent roster, brand feeds), Understand (AI reads captions, audio and frames in 12 languages), Match (seven-part fit score after hard gates), Forecast (views, TG reach, clicks, orders, recall, cost per outcome, with a band), Verify (plugs into Chtrbox's real-time tracking, dedupes, bot-filters, nets out returns, runs holdouts), Learn (forecast error retrains the model; "MAPE 16–20% on the sample"). The footer repeats the simulated-data disclosure.

Say it like this

On the right, the hollow dot is the ROI an agency usually quotes using EMV. The pink dot is what a holdout test proves. For Amazon that's 3.36× claimed against 1.74× proven. I'd rather walk into a CMO's office with 1.74× I can defend."

Real and active followers below 72%; brand-safety score below 55; a post for a named competitor in the last 60 days; audience outside the campaign market; finance content without SEBI registration (for SEBI-regulated brands); health claims without a medical or nutrition qualification (for health brands). Geo holdout

Run the campaign in some cities and hold it back in matched cities. The difference in conversion rate is the part the creators caused. Lift = (2.98 − 1.87) ÷ 1.87 = 59.4%.

MAPE (mean absolute percentage error)

The average size of the forecast miss, ignoring direction. 19.4% means forecasts of views were off by about a fifth on average. The rollout targets below 30% by day 45 and below 15% by month 12.
