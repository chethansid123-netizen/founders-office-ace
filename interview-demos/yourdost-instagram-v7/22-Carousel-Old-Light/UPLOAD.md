# Post 22: Checking in on people who look fine

**The line:** The starlight you see tonight left its star years ago.
**Why it works:** It opens on a true, slightly mind-bending fact under a beautiful sky, which is why people swipe and save. Then it turns: people work the same way. The ‘all good!’ text and the trip photos are old light. It ends with the simplest action, ask how they are tonight.
