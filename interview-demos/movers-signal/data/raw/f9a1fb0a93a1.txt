Dear Sir/Madam,
In accordance with Regulation 30 read with Schedule Il of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 ("Listing Regulations"), we would like to inform you that Nomination
and Remuneration Committee has today, approved following in respect of the Senior Management
Personnel (‘SMP”) of the Company:
1. Appointment of Dr. Ashish Kumar Pandey: Dr. Ashish Kumar Pandey has been appointed as
Assistant Vice President — Operations (Cluster Head — Unit 2 & 3) designated as Senior
Management Personnel of the Company, with effect from November 2, 2026.
2. Declassification of Senior Management Personnel: Consequent to the above mentioned
appointment, Mr. Anurag Shukla, General Manager - Operations (Head Unit 2) and Mr. Uday
Desai, General Manager -Operations (Head Unit 3) who are designated as Senior Management
Personnel of Company, shall henceforth report to Dr. Ashish Kumar Pandey. Accordingly, due to
change in internal reporting structure, Mr. Anurag Shukla and Mr. Uday Desai shall cease to be
designated as Senior Management Personnel of the Company with effect from November 2,
2026. However since there is no cessation of employment, Mr. Anurag Shukla and Mr. Uday Desai
will be continuing in the Company under the new reporting structure.
