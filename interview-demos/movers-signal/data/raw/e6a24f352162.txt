Annexure A
Vivek Mehta has been appointed as Chief Internal Auditor and Head - Internal Assurance
(Senior Management) of the Company effective August 5, 2026, in place of Ramjee Verma
who will be moving into a new role within the Group w.e.f. August 5, 2026. The brief profile of
Vivek Mehta is as under:
“Vivek Mehta is currently associated as Global Shared Service Head and Senior Vice
President - Finance of the Company. A seasoned professional, Vivek brings over more than
two decades of diverse experience in financial leadership, particularly within the
telecommunications sector. Before joining Airtel, he was with VIP Industries Ltd. Vivek
Mehta’s career at Airtel is marked by progressive leadership roles at center, including his
previous role as Head - Regulatory Compliance. In his current role as Global Finance Shared
Service Head and Senior Vice President at Bharti Airtel, Vivek Mehta is leading initiatives on
automating and simplifying the financial processes across geographies for the Company.
Throughout his tenure at Bharti Airtel, Vivek has demonstrated capabilities in operational
excellence, strengthening governance, regulatory compliance & large team handling.”
The aforesaid change was approved/ taken on record by the Board of Directors, in its meeting
held today i.e. August 4, 2026 which commenced at IST 1400 Hrs. and concluded at IST 1740
Hrs.
