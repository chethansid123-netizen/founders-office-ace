Pursuant to the requirements of Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the Listing Regulations, we wish to
inform you that the Board of Directors of Info Edge (India) Limited (“Company”), at its meeting held today, i.e.,
October 28, 2026, have, inter alia, considered and approved the following:
1. Transition of Mr. Hitesh Oberoi from full-time operating role
The Board of Directors took note of the decision of Mr. Hitesh Oberoi (DIN: 01189953) to step down from his
positions as Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer (Key Managerial Personnel) of the Company, with
effect from the close of business hours on December 2, 2026, after nearly 27 years with Info Edge. This decision
has been taken by Mr. Oberoi after careful consideration, in order to devote more time to his personal interests
in education, entrepreneurship, institution building, investing and philanthropy, as well as to spend more time
with his family.
The Board places on record its appreciation for Mr. Oberoi’s significant contributions to the Company over
nearly 27 years and his role in building and strengthening its businesses and leadership team.
2. Appointment of Mr. Hitesh Oberoi as Additional Director
Based on the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board of Directors
approved the appointment of Mr. Hitesh Oberoi (DIN: 01189953) as an Additional Director, designated as a
Non-Executive, Non-Independent Director under the Promoter Category, liable to retire by rotation, with effect
from December 3, 2026, subject to the approval of the members of the Company.
Mr. Oberoi’s appointment will enable the Board to continue to benefit from his extensive experience,
institutional knowledge and deep understanding of the Company’s businesses.

Date: October 27, 2026
To,
The Board of Directors
Info Edge (India) Limited
Subject: Resignation from the positions of Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer
Dear Board Members,
After nearly 27 years at Info Edge, including over 16 years as MD and CEO, I have decided that it is time
for me to step back from my full-time operating role. Accordingly, I hereby tender my resignation from
the positions of Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer of the Company with effect from the
close of business on December 2, 2026.
I have thought about this decision for some time, and it has not been an easy one to make. Info Edge
has been a defining part of my life, and I feel enormous gratitude and pride when I look back at what we
have built together.
Over the years, there were a few things I wanted to see through before I felt comfortable stepping back,
and I am happy to say that I believe we have got there.
Naukri is where our journey began. I still remember the early days, when we raised money just in time,
set up a sales team, launched Resdex, and for several years made 60-70 sales calls a month alongside
the team so that we could break even quickly. Today, Naukri has a clear Al and Data strategy which is
playing out well and will help power its next phase of growth.
99acres, which tested our patience and resolve for many years, has established clear market leadership
over the last couple of years and is now a robust, cash-generating business. Jeevansathi and Aisle
continue to gain market share and are now profitable, while Jobhai has built a credible monetisation
engine and laid the foundations for a much larger business. Watching each of these businesses grow,
often through difficult phases, has been one of the great joys of my working life.
More importantly, together, we have built a much stronger institution. We have senior leaders who
have been with Info Edge for over 20 years and understand our businesses deeply, alongside a younger
generation of leaders who are ambitious and ready to take on greater responsibility. Many of them I
have had the privilege of working alongside for several years, and some I have seen grow from their first
jobs into great leaders.
If there is one thing I am most proud of, it is the quality of the team we have built at Info Edge. I will miss
working with them enormously; the energy, camaraderie and sense of purpose that have made coming
to work at Info Edge so rewarding. Together, we have built businesses that generate substantial cash,
which has allowed Info Edge to evolve into an important investor in, and supporter of, India's startup

ecosystem. I believe the organisation is now at a point where it has the depth of leadership and
institutional strength to move confidently into its next phase.
I therefore feel this is the right time for me to step back.
This is not a decision to step away from Info Edge. I remain deeply invested in the company, both
emotionally and financially. Subject to shareholder approval, I will be happy to continue to serve on the
Board as a Non-Executive Director, and I will remain available to help whenever needed.
In the next phase of my life, I want to make more time to pursue my interests in higher education,
entrepreneurship, investing and philanthropy. I would also like to spend more time with my family.
Without their rock solid support, I would not have made it so far.
I would like to thank all our past and present Board Members, especially Ashish Gupta, Arun Duggal,
Sha rad Malik, Bala Deshpande, Saurabh Srivastava, and Naresh Gupta, for their support and guidance
over the years, particularly when Info Edge was a small company. I am grateful to my colleagues, past
and present especially Ambarish who came back to support us again as interim CFO, to our shareholders
for their trust, and to Sanjeev, for his long partnership.
I leave my full-time operating role with a heavy heart and enormous gratitude, proud of what we have
built and excited about what lies ahead.
Y urs sincerely,

PRESS RELEASE
Info Edge Announces Leadership Transition
Hitesh Oberoi to transition from MD & CEO role after nearly 27 years; Sanjeev Bikhchandani to assume
expanded executive leadership role.
Info Edge (India) Ltd. (NSE: NAUKRI; BSE: 532777) today announced a leadership transition following
decisions taken by its Board of Directors.
Mr. Hitesh Oberoi, Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer of Info Edge, will step down from his full-
time executive role with effect from the close of business on December 2, 2026, after nearly 27 years with
the Company, including over 16 years as MD & CEO.
Subject to shareholders’ approval, Mr. Oberoi will continue his association with Info Edge as a Non-
Executive, Non-Independent Director with effect from December 3, 2026.
The Board has approved the appointment of Mr. Sanjeev Bikhchandani, Founder and Vice Chairman of Info
Edge, as Executive Vice Chairman, Managing Director and Group Chief Executive Officer, with effect from
December 3, 2026, subject to shareholders’ approval.
Over the last 27 years, Mr. Oberoi and has played a central role in building the Company into one of India’s
leading consumer internet businesses. During his tenure as MD and CEO, Naukri.com strengthened its
position as India’s leading online recruitment platform, while Info Edge built and scaled businesses
including 99acres, Jeevansathi, Shiksha and Jobhai and invested its surplus cash in India’s startup
ecosystem.
Over his 16 years as Chief Executive Officer, Info Edge’s market capitalisation grew from approximately
₹2,500 crore to approximately ₹80,000 crore. During this period, the Company also developed a broad
leadership team across its businesses, creating a strong institutional foundation for the next phase of
growth.
Mr. Hitesh Oberoi on his transition
“After nearly 27 years at Info Edge, I feel this is the right time for me to begin transitioning out of my full-
time operating role. It has not been an easy decision. Info Edge has been a defining part of my life, and I
feel enormous gratitude and pride when I look back at what we have built together.
There were a few things I wanted to see through before stepping back. Today, Naukri has a clear AI and
Data strategy which is playing out well and will help power its next phase of growth. 99acres has
established strong market leadership over the last couple of years and is now a fast growing, robust cash
generating business. Jeevansathi and Aisle continue to gain market share and are now profitable and Jobhai
has built a credible monetisation engine and laid the foundations for a much larger business.
More importantly, together, we have built a much stronger institution. We have senior leaders who have
been with Info Edge for over 20 years and understand our businesses deeply, alongside a younger
