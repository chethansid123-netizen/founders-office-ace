Profile of Mr. Anish Kumar Sarkar – Chief Executive Officer
Anish Sarkar is a seasoned executive leader with over two decades of experience in business
building, transformation and M&A-led growth across consulting and operational leadership
roles. Prior to joining NIIT, he was Managing Director – India and Director of the India /
Philippines / Indonesia Cluster at Pluxee (formerly Sodexo BRS), a global employee benefits
and engagement company; and CEO of Mercer India, a global leader in human capital
consulting. At both these organisations, he led strong revenue growth and portfolio expansion,
including strategic acquisitions.
Earlier, Anish spent several years with the Capgemini Group in senior leadership roles
spanning management consulting, sales and technology transformation. Across these roles,
he built deep experience in growth strategy, client partnerships, digital transformation and
technology-led business growth. An alumnus of IIT Kharagpur and IIM Calcutta, Anish brings
a distinctive blend of business, human capital and technology expertise to NIIT.
