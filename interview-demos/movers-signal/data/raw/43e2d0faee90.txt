Annexure-A
Change in Group Chief Financial Officer (CFO) & Key Managerial Personnel (KMP):
The Board noted the resignation of Mr. Alexander Poempner as Group CFO & KMP of the Company, effective at
the conclusion of the Board Meeting at which the unaudited financial results for the quarter and half year ended
September 30, 2026 (Q2 FY27) will be approved, currently planned for November 4, 2026.

ensure an orderly handover and provide transition support.

conclusion of the said Board Meeting based on the recommendations of the Nomination & Remuneration
Committee and the Audit Committee.

contributions to the Company and wishes him all the best for the future.
