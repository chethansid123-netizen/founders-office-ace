Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (‘Listing Regulations’), we
hereby inform you about the following changes in Senior Management Personnel of the Company:
1. Resignation of Mr. Vivek Kulkarni, Executive Vice President – Ingredients Division:
Mr. Vivek Kulkarni, Executive Vice President – Ingredients Division and Senior Management Personnel of the
Company, has resigned from the services of the Company to pursue other professional interests outside the Keva
Group. He is being relieved from the services with effect from the close of business hours of September 30, 2026.
2. Appointment of Mr. Rajeev Mishra as Vice President – Sales & Ingredients Division:
Consequent to Mr. Vivek Kulkarni’s decision to resign from the Company and as part of re-orgnisation within the
Group, the Board of Directors of the Company, basis the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration
Committee, has today i.e. on September 30, 2026 approved the appointment of Mr. Rajeev Mishra [currently serving as
Associate Vice President – Strategy (Fragrance & AID)] as Vice President – Sales & Ingredients Division and Senior
Management Personnel of the Company with effect from October 01, 2026.
The approval of the Board of Directors was received at 02:33 p.m. (IST).
The details as required under Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations read with Part A of Schedule III of the Listing
