including leadership and strategic vision, depth of banking and financial services experience, exposure to large scale operations,
risk management acumen, regulatory and compliance orientation, stakeholder management capability, governance track record,
digital and technology transformation experience and overall understanding of the Bank’s business model and long term priorities.

in terms of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks - Governance) Directions, 2025 and
other relevant directions, circulars and notifications issued by the Reserve Bank of India (“RBI”) from time to time, the Listing
Regulations and the Policy on Appointment and Fit and Proper Criteria for Directors of the Bank, along with other applicable rules

shortlisted by the Bank, for the appointment of Mr. Anup Bagchi as the MD & CEO of the Bank, for a period of 3 (three) years,
commencing from October 27, 2026 till October 26, 2029.
Mr. Anup Bagchi is a seasoned financial services leader with over three decades of experience across banking, capital markets,
wealth management and insurance. A member of the ICICI Group since 1992, he has built a diverse career spanning treasury, retail
banking, wholesale banking, investment banking, and digital financial services. Over the course of his career, he has held several
pivotal leadership positions, including CEO & MD of ICICI Securities Limited, Executive Director of ICICI Bank Limited, and currently
is the MD & CEO of ICICI Life Insurance Limited (formerly known as ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Limited). His career
reflects a strong track record of business building, strategic leadership, and navigating the evolving financial services landscape.
Mr. Bagchi served as an Executive Director of ICICI Bank Limited from 2017 to 2023, overseeing Retail, Business and Rural Banking,
and subsequently Wholesale Banking. During his tenure, he played a key role in strengthening the ICICI Bank’s retail franchise,
driving profitable growth while maintaining credit and cost discipline. Under his leadership, ICICI Bank Limited became the first
private-sector bank in India to surpass a retail mortgage portfolio of Rs. 2 trillion and successfully navigated the demonetisation
period with limited business disruption. Alongside his executive responsibilities, he served as Chairman of ICICI Prudential Asset
Management Company Limited until May 2023 and was a Director of ICICI Prudential Pension Fund Management Limited and
ICICI Home Finance Company Limited.
Since June 2023, Mr. Bagchi has been serving as the MD & CEO of ICICI Life Insurance Limited, leading one of India’s largest
private life insurers, which is publicly listed. Under his leadership, the company crossed Rs. 10,000 crore in Annualised Premium
Equivalent (APE) for the first time in FY2025, with APE growing 15% year-on-year to Rs. 10,407 crore and profit after tax increasing
nearly 40% to Rs. 1,189 crore. The business has also delivered strong growth in protection products and new business generation,
reflecting his focus on sustainable and profitable expansion.
