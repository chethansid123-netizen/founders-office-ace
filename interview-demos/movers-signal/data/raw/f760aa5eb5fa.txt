Sub: Outcome of the Board Meeting held on Monday, November 2, 2026
Dear Sir/Madam,
Further to our letter dated November 2, 2026, submitted pursuant to Regulations 30 and 33 of the SEBI
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, regarding the unaudited
consolidated and standalone financial results of the Company for the quarter and half year ended
September 30, 2026, we wish to further inform you that the Board of Directors (“Board”) of the Company,
at its meeting held today, i.e., Monday, November 2, 2026, inter alia, considered and approved the
following additional matters:
1. Appointment of Statutory Auditors:
The Company's existing Statutory Auditors, B S R & Co. LLP, Chartered Accountants (ICAI Firm
Registration No. 101248W/W-100022), will complete their second term as Statutory Auditors of the
Company at the conclusion of the 43rd Annual General Meeting (“AGM”) of the Company. Based on
the recommendation of the Audit Committee, the Board approved the appointment of S R B C & CO
LLP, Chartered Accountants (ICAI Firm Registration No. 324982E/E300003), as the Statutory
Auditors of the Company for a term of 5 (five) consecutive years, commencing from the conclusion
of the 43rd AGM to be held in the year 2027 until the conclusion of the 48th AGM to be held in the
year 2032, subject to the approval of the Members at the ensuing AGM.
The details required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations, read with SEBI Master
Circular No. SEBI/HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-PoD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026, are
enclosed as Annexure I.
2. Appointment of Mr. Shatmanyu Shrivastava (DIN: 12023367) as an Additional Director (Non-
Executive, Non-Independent):
Based on the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board
approved the appointment of Mr. Shatmanyu Shrivastava (DIN: 12023367) as an Additional Director
(Non-Executive, Non-Independent), nominated by Life Insurance Corporation of India (“LIC”), with
effect from November 2, 2026. The same is subject to the approval of the Members of the Company.
His appointment will not only facilitate stakeholder representation on the Board but also enhance
Board diversity. Mr. Shrivastava has 36 plus years’ strong experience of working at various senior
positions in LIC, which predominantly includes heading the Finance and Risk Management
functions.
3. Appointment of Senior Management Personnel:
Based on the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board
approved the appointment of Mr. Sameer Tandon, President - APAC, Africa and Europe, as Senior
Management Personnel of the Company with effect from November 2, 2026.
