Dear Sir/Madam,
In continuation to our earlier intimation dated October 12, 2026, and pursuant to Regulation
30, Schedule III, Part A, Para A (7) and other applicable provisions of the SEBI Listing
Regulations, as amended, this is to inform you that the Board of Directors of RBL Bank Limited
(“the Bank”) at its meeting held today i.e. on October 12, 2026, has, inter-alia noted/approved
the following:
I. Changes in Senior Management of the Bank:
1. Mr. Deepak Kumar - Chief Risk Officer (CRO), being part of the Senior Management of
RBL Bank Limited (“the Bank”) has been associated with the Bank on a fixed term contract.
As per the terms, the contract shall conclude on October 31, 2026. Accordingly,
Mr. Deepak Kumar shall cease to be a part of the Senior Management of the Bank
effective close of business hours on October 31, 2026.
2. Further, we wish to inform you that basis the recommendations of the Risk Management
Committee of the Board and Nomination and Remuneration Committee of the Board, the
Board approved appointment of Mr. Ashwini Kumar Choudhary as the Chief Risk Officer
of the Bank with effect from November 01, 2026. Mr. Ashwini Kumar Choudhary is a part
of the Senior Management of the Bank.
The intimation with respect to change in incumbency (Sr. no. 1 and 2) is filed in compliance
with Chapter III F of the Reserve Bank of India (“RBI”) Directions Ref No. RBI/DOR/2025-
26/149 DOR.HGG.GOV. No.68/29.67.001/2025-26 dated November 28, 2025.
3. Further, we wish to inform you that basis the recommendations of the Audit Committee of
the Board and Nomination and Remuneration Committee of the Board, the Board
approved the re-appointment of Mr. Prakash Gupta as the Chief Compliance Officer of the
Bank with effect from November 1, 2026 for a period of one year eight months.
Mr. Prakash Gupta is a part of the Senior Management of the Bank.
