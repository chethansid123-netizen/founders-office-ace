4. How I take ownership, stay disciplined, and keep focus

Ownership: say it like this

"For me, ownership means I treat the outcome as mine even when the team isn't. I write down what 'done' means and the KPI, I name an owner for every step, and I track it daily until it closes. If something slips, I escalate early with a fix, not just a problem. At Techvaraha that's how on-time delivery went from 65% to 90%."

Discipline: my system

  • Write it down first: SOPs, KRAs and checklists before scaling (5+ at Techvaraha, high-value scheme SOPs at LVX).
  • One source of truth: a dashboard or tracker for every pipeline, so nobody relies on memory.
  • Fixed rhythm: daily blocker check, weekly review with owners, and a monthly look-back on KPIs.
  • Speed with a deadline: 0 → 1 blueprints in under 72 hours, then iterate. I don't wait for perfect.
  • Async by design: clear hand-offs across Bangalore, Gurugram and Dubai cut escalation time 40%.

Focus: say it like this

"I keep focus by choosing the one number that matters for the quarter, like on-time delivery or deals closed, and saying no to work that doesn't move it. I protect the founder's bandwidth by taking the operational load off their plate in week one. And outside work, long rides and runs (I ran the 10K Founder Run) keep my head clear."

A time things went wrong (have this ready)

Use the Techvaraha delivery problem: "Delivery was 65% on time. Instead of blaming teams, I found the root cause: no shared definition of 'done' and no owners. I built SOPs, QA workflows and KRAs, and within the period on-time delivery reached 90%."
