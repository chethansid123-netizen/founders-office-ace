Workings, live in the model

Store sales: units a week, times 52, divided by 12, times MRP

We receive: MRP less GST, retailer margin and distributor margin

We keep: receipts less kg times all costs, less promotions

EBITDA: a 6% start plus each lever’s bps times its ramp

Modern trade revenue: average stores times receipts a store a month, times 12

Quick commerce revenue: average dark stores times ₹470, times 365

Freight a kg: km times ₹38 times 1.5, divided by 1,800 kg, plus ₹1.5

Money tied up: sales times days, divided by 365, at 11% a year
