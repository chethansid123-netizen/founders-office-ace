PLAN AND NUMBERS (90 seconds). “Three phases, each with a gate, so we never scale on hope. Pilot, January to March 2027: five Guwahati stores and two controls, the eight wave-1 SKUs, a shelf audit and 300 shopper intercepts, and the purchase team buys gram at harvest. Gate: two of five stores at plan for two four-week cycles, a 95% fill rate and at least ₹40 a kilo after promotions. Prove, April 2027 to March 2028: 25 stores across Assam, all 12 SKUs, better terms from store six, and quick commerce in about 30 Guwahati dark stores. Gate: EBITDA at 7% or more for two quarters, and a store payback within six months. Propagate, FY29 to FY31: the 74 Vishal stores in the Northeast, then 120 with North Bengal, then 180 with East chains. Quick commerce from 120 to 400 dark stores, adding Kolkata and Siliguri. Gate: each new chain must reach ₹36,000 a store a month. Quick commerce is second, not first: only basket-building packs, never ₹5 or ₹10 singles, because picking and the 20–23% fee kill them. A 400-gram pack still leaves about ₹51 a kilo after a 22% fee and 6% ads. At about six units a dark store a day, that is ₹470 of net sales per dark store per day. We watch search rank, availability, ratings and repeat buyers. The numbers, Base case: revenue grows with the market, 8% in FY26 and then 10% a year, from ₹311 crore to about ₹501 crore by FY31. Modern trade and quick commerce go from about 2% of sales to about 4%, roughly ₹19 crore. EBITDA goes from 6% to 8% by FY29 and 8.4% by FY31, from about ₹19 crore to about ₹42 crore. If MT works better than plan, the model shows what 500 stores at ₹45,000 a month would add, about ₹18 crore a year. Everything here is live in the Google Sheet; switch Slow, Base or Fast in one cell. What I track weekly: units per SKU per store, fill rate, stock-outs, rupees per kilo after promotions and receivable days. Monthly: EBITDA.” Engagement: “If you had to pick one number to see on my first Monday, which would it be?” Transition: “The last page lists every source, the links and how each number is worked out.”
