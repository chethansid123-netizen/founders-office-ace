THE FOUNDER’S OFFICE PLAN (60 s). “Here is how I’d spend my first 90 days — watch the bar fill. Days 1 to 30, diagnose: one fact base — sales by SKU, channel and outlet, plant use by line — and I ride ten beats with the sales reps to see the shelf myself. The gate: the founders agree the numbers. Days 31 to 60, prioritise: pick the 25 to 30 hero SKUs, list the ones to cut or merge, and size and cost the three pilots. The gate: pilots and budget signed off. Days 61 to 90, execute: launch the general-trade, modern-trade and quick-commerce pilots, review weekly with the founders, and write the playbook. The gate: each pilot is scaled, modified or stopped on its numbers. All of it runs on one weekly founder dashboard: revenue by month and state, channel mix, SKU performance — where the top 30 carry most of the sales — outlet productivity against a target, gross versus contribution margin, inventory and stock-outs, each pilot against its control, and an initiative tracker by owner and status. The shapes are illustrative; the values come from your data, not my guesses. The objective is not simply to grow revenue. It is to build repeatable, profitable growth through better consumer understanding, stronger distribution productivity and disciplined execution. And my question back to you: which of these numbers do you already track every week?”
