THE FOUNDER’S OFFICE PLAN (60 s). “Here is how I would spend my first 90 days. Days 1 to 30, diagnose: build one fact base — sales by SKU, channel and outlet; plant use by line; margin per SKU and per channel — and ride ten beats with the sales reps to see the shelf myself. Days 31 to 60, prioritise: pick the 25 to 30 hero SKUs, list the ones to cut or merge, size and cost the three pilots, and agree owners and KPIs with the founders. Days 61 to 90, execute: launch the general-trade, modern-trade and quick-commerce pilots, review weekly with the founders, and scale, modify or stop each one on the numbers — then write the playbook. All of it runs on one dashboard: revenue, channel sales, SKU performance, outlet productivity, gross and contribution margin, inventory and stock-outs, pilot results against control, and an initiative tracker. I’ve left the tiles empty on purpose — the values come from your data, not my guesses. Closing line: the objective is not simply to grow revenue. It is to build repeatable, profitable growth through better consumer understanding, stronger distribution productivity and disciplined execution. And my question back to you: which of these numbers do you already track every week?”
