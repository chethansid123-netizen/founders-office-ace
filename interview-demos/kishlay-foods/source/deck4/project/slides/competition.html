COMPETITION AND INDUSTRY PRESSURE (90 s). “Kishlay isn’t competing in one market. It’s fighting three battles on the same crowded shelf. Battle one, national players — PepsiCo with Lay’s, Kurkure and Cheetos; ITC with Bingo!, Sunfeast and YiPPee!; Nestlé with Maggi; Britannia, Parle, Haldiram’s, Bikaji and Mrs. Bectors. They win on brand recall, advertising, procurement scale and national retail relationships. Kishlay’s answer: it makes locally, so it can be fresher and cheaper to serve in the Northeast. Battle two, East and Northeast players — the key fight: PRAN, Prataap’s Yellow Diamond, Gopal, Balaji, Anmol, Bisk Farm, Annapurna Swadisht, CG Foods’ Wai Wai, DFM’s Crax, Guiltfree’s Too Yumm! and AFP’s Munchh Onn. Same shopper, same ₹5, local taste. Kishlay’s answer: taste, the ₹5 pack and more productive outlets. Battle three, local and unorganised — Ridhi Sidhi’s Motu Rama, Chittchore Namkeenz, Assam and Bengal bakeries, loose chanachur and street food like jhalmuri, samosa and pakora. They win on proximity and low overhead; Kishlay’s answer is trusted brand, same ₹5. Bottom left, category by category, who Kishlay meets on the shelf, with the only price points I can confirm: Lay’s, Yellow Diamond rings, Crax rings and Wai Wai at ₹5, Britannia cookies at ₹35, Haldiram’s bhujia at ₹110 — these are examples, not their full price ladders. Bottom right, the five forces, each with one piece of evidence: rivalry, suppliers and buyers are high — 130-plus tracked rivals, inputs at 70–75% of cost per CRISIL, and a price fixed at ₹5 and ₹10; substitutes are medium-high; entrants medium. Three nuances if asked: PepsiCo’s Frito-Lay and Britannia have also hired Kishlay to co-pack, so they are partners as well as rivals; there are look-alike disputes — Mrs. Bectors over NON STOP biscuits and Motu Rama copying NON STOP noodle packs; and D2C brands like Open Secret, Go Desi, True Elements, Gladful and Yours Freshly are benchmarks for packaging and online selling, not core general-trade rivals. I haven’t compared competitor factories or distributor counts because I don’t have verified data.” Transition: “If the ₹5 shelf is this crowded, where can Kishlay build a stronger position?”
