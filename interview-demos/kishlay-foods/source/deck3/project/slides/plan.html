WHAT I’D DO (75–90 s). “Four limits, each with one number and one fix. Inputs: 70–75% of cost on a coin price — when wheat, sugar and palm oil spiked in FY22 the group lost ₹20.7 Cr — so write a grammage playbook before the next spike. SKUs: ₹344 Cr over 900-plus SKUs is about ₹38 lakh of sales per SKU a year — cut the tail and make 25–30 heroes must-stock. Capacity: roughly 50–75% of 2,600 MT a month used — load the hub first and make each SKU at its nearest plant. Cash: about 79% of bank limits used and a current ratio around one; CRISIL noted in June 2026 that monthly information wasn’t submitted — a weekly MIS fixes both. One metric: sales per active outlet, from about ₹17k toward ₹23k — half the gap to Gopal — which is roughly ₹120 Cr a year on the outlets Kishlay already has. Ninety days: diagnose in the first 30 — the SKU cube, outlet productivity by beat, freight per kilo by lane; focus in 31–60 — must-stock heroes, cut the tail, the grammage plan; execute in 61–90 — bigger drops with ₹20–55 packs, weekly beat reviews, nearest-plant making; then scale over months 4–12 — a Guwahati quick-commerce pilot (Blinkit has served Guwahati since January 2025), copy winning beats into Bihar and Bengal via Hajipur, and Bhutan. What I’d own: one weekly plant-to-outlet MIS, the SKU cube, and the quick-commerce pilot P&L.” Then ask: “Which of these four limits keeps you up at night?”
