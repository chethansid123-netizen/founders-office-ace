PACK AND PRICE (60 s). “Six pack formats, each solving a physical problem: nitrogen-flushed metallised pouches keep ₹5 and ₹10 snacks crisp through the monsoon; a 400 g zipper pouch at ₹110 keeps family namkeen fresh; cartons for Choco Tik, corn flakes and the 180 g Nutri Zing box; sachets for pickles, imli chutney and ketchup; tetra packs and PET for drinks; flow-wrap for muffins and cake. Every product sits on four coin tiers: ₹5 impulse — rings with toys, puffs, a 60 g eggless muffin, a 12.5 g Gold Tea sachet; ₹10 on the go — Cocktail Peanut, Zing chips and noodles, slice cake, 25 g tea; ₹20–55 sharing — Potato Feast ₹20, rusk ₹30–40, cookies ₹35–55, 200 g namkeen ₹55; ₹110–250 family — 400 g bhujia ₹110, Gulab Jamun ₹230, Rasgulla ₹250. Because the coin can’t move, grams do: that Masala Noodles pack says 25% extra at the same ₹5. Shelf life sets how far a pack can travel: muffins one month, rasgulla one to two, cookies and Gulab Jamun three to four, rusk five, namkeen and noodles six to nine, tea and cheese bhujia twelve. And 236 products times up to eight pack sizes is how you reach 900-plus SKUs.”
