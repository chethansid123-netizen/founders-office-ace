THE RANGE (45–60 s). “On the left, every product on the website as one picture, box size by count: 236 products in 18 categories. 184 of them — nearly four in five — are savoury: chips and snacks 66, namkeen 39, fryms 19, other snacks 16, higher-MRP snacks 11, sticks 8, rings 7, potato chips 6, balls 5, popcorn 4, puffs 3. Bakery is 22 — cookies 12, cakes and muffins 10. Pantry 25 — pickles and ketchup 10, cereals 6, noodles 6, soya 3. Drinks 5. In the middle, the same range as four shelf families: chips and western snacks 72 at ₹5, ₹10 and ₹20; extruded snacks and fryms 73, almost all ₹5; namkeen, noodles and soya 48, from ₹5 sachets to the ₹110 400 g pouch; bakery and pantry 43, ₹5 to ₹55. And a big part of the business isn’t on the website at all: 31 Mamooz biscuit varieties, Bisk Baker rusk at ₹30 and ₹40, Assam tea in ₹5 and ₹10 sachets, and boxed sweets at ₹230 and ₹250. Breadth wins distributors — one supplier fills 18 slots — but the long tail ties up stock and cash.”
