INSIDE THE HUB (60 s). “Left to right: the hub, its lines, and the verdict. North Guwahati is 2,00,000-plus sq ft in Brahmaputra Industrial Park; every pack passes six checks — raw material, in-process, lab, packaging, sensory, pre-dispatch — under ISO 22000, FSSAI and HACCP. Four lines: potato chips on stainless PLC lines from Siddhivinayak Agri Processing, 200 to 1,500 kg an hour; Fabcon extruders for rings, sticks and pasta shapes; a balls and puffs line; and biscuits for Mamooz and cookies. Sealed screw conveyors move flour and spice; Pace and Astro high-speed machines pack. People: Tulsi has 512 staff, three in four blue-collar, and a January 2026 study found absence peaks in July — exactly when monsoon snacking peaks. The verdict: capacity is about 2,600 MT a month, 31,200 a year. If I divide ₹344 Cr of FY24 sales by a reasonable ₹150–220 a kilo, they sold 15,600 to 22,900 tonnes — so roughly 50 to 75% of capacity is used. The factory isn’t the limit; demand is.” Invite correction on the real utilisation.
