WHERE TO PLAY (60–75 s). Two pictures. “On the left, the market split by price and reach. The top-left, shaded red, is the red ocean: Kishlay, Pran, Annapurna, Wai Wai, Bisk Farm, Balaji, Gopal and loose local namkeen all fight for the ₹5 strip. The nationals — PepsiCo, ITC, Britannia, Parle, Haldiram’s, Bikaji, Yellow Diamond — sit lower. The top-right, regional premium, is empty: that’s the blue ocean, and Kishlay’s range already stretches to ₹250 with flavours no national makes. To get there: eliminate look-alike SKUs, reduce new ₹5 shapes, raise regional flavours at ₹55–110, and create an NE range for export. On the right, Ansoff puts growth in order. One, penetration — more per Northeast outlet, from about ₹17k toward ₹23k a year with must-stock heroes and bigger drops; lowest risk. Two, the same packs in new markets — Bihar and Bengal via the Hajipur plant, Bhutan and Nepal, and quick commerce in Guwahati. Three, new regional premium products — Jolokia, Jhalmuri and 40 Aana in ₹55–110 packs, and makhana. Four, diversification — not now; with 18 categories already, the business needs focus.” Map positions are placed on price lists and reach.
