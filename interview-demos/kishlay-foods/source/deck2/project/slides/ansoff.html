ANSOFF (45–60 s). “So where should growth come from? In priority order. One — market penetration: existing products in existing markets. Get more out of the two lakh outlets Kishlay already has: hero SKUs in every shop and bigger drops, taking sales per outlet from about ₹17k toward ₹23k. Lowest risk, fastest payback. Two — market development: the same packs on new ground — Bihar and Bengal through the Hajipur plant, Bhutan and Nepal exports, and quick commerce in Guwahati, where Blinkit has operated since January 2025. Three — product development: regional premium, like Jolokia and Jhalmuri in ₹55–110 packs, and makhana or roasted lines, which is the blue pocket from the canvas. Four — diversification: not now. With 18 categories already, the business needs focus, not more categories.”
