HUB-AND-SPOKE (45–60 s). Trace it with your finger, top to bottom. “Four raw materials — wheat, sugar, potato and corn, palm oil — are 70 to 75% of cost. They go into three plant sites: the own hub in North Guwahati, 2,000 MT, with third-party plants at Hajipur in Bihar and in Chhattisgarh at 300 MT a month each, each sitting inside the market it serves. Tulsi Speciality Foods makes — 512 people — and hands over to Kishlay Foods, which owns the brands and sells. And it goes out four ways: two lakh-plus general-trade outlets in 17-plus states, BigBasket, exports to Bhutan, and co-packing for Frito-Lay and Britannia.” The why in one line: making near the eater beats trucking air across the Siliguri Corridor.
