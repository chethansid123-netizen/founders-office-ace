PRICE-PACK STAIRCASE (45 s). “Every product sits on one of four coin steps. ₹5 impulse — rings with toys, fryums, a 60 g eggless muffin, a 12.5 g Gold Tea sachet, Choco Tik. ₹10 on the go — Cocktail Peanut, Zing chips and noodles, slice cake. ₹20 to ₹55 for sharing — Potato Feast at ₹20, rusk at ₹30–40, cookies at ₹35–55, 200 g namkeen at ₹55. ₹110 to ₹250 for family and festive — 400 g zipper bhujia at ₹110, Gulab Jamun ₹230, Rasgulla ₹250. On the bottom two steps the price is a coin and never moves; the grams do — that Masala Noodles pack says 25% extra at the same ₹5. And because every product comes in several of eight pack sizes — ₹5, ₹10, ₹20, 50, 110, 180, 200 and 400 g — 236 products become 900-plus SKUs.”
