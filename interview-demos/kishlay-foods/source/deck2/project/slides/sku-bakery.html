That adds up to the 236. But a big part of the range isn’t on the website at all: Mamooz biscuits — 31 varieties listed on IndiaMART, from 20 Plus and Golden Marie to Elaichi Cream and Nasta — Bisk Baker rusk at ₹30 and ₹40, Assam tea from a ₹5 12.5 g sachet up to 250 g cardamom packs, and boxed sweets — Rasgulla ₹250, Gulab Jamun ₹230, Soan Papdi.” First principles: bakery has the shortest shelf life (muffins about a month, cookies three to four), so it only works on tight beats near the plant; tea and sweets are long-life and occasion-driven. The fact that the catalogue online, the 292+ SKU figure and the 900+ figure all differ is itself a finding: there is no single master SKU list in public, and probably not one clean one internally either.
