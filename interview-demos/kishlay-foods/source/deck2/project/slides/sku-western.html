SKUs 1 OF 4 — POTATO CHIPS AND WESTERN SNACKS (45–60 s). Don’t read the list; point at it. “This is the full list from the website — 72 products: 6 under Potato Chips and 66 under Chips & Snacks. I’ve grouped the 66 by how they’re made, because that’s what matters operationally: 11 potato-based — the Zing range, Flames, Crinkle Chips, Potato Feast at ₹20, Potato Snacky, Laccha Paratha; 10 ring variants; 21 fried pellets and shapes — Masala Noodles, Sizzling Noodles, pasta, pipe, katori, poochka, squares, Hot Wheels, Ludo, Firki Master; 11 sticks, puffs and balls; 3 choco snacks; and 10 mixes and kids’ packs.” The insight: the potato-based items need the chips lines and fresh potato; everything else comes off extruders and pellet fryers, where adding a shape is cheap. So variety is cheap to make but costly to stock — 21 pellet shapes is where an SKU-velocity cut would start.
