CONSTRAINTS AND CLOSE (45–60 s). “Four limits, each with one number and one fix. Inputs: 70–75% of cost is commodities on a fixed coin MRP — in FY22 the wheat, sugar and palm-oil spike meant a ₹20.7 Cr loss — so the fix is a grammage playbook written before the next spike. SKUs: about ₹38 lakh of sales per SKU a year by my maths — cut the tail and make 25–30 heroes must-stock. Capacity: roughly 50–75% used — load the hub first and make each SKU at its nearest plant. Cash and reporting: about 78–79% of bank limits used and a current ratio around one; CRISIL noted in June 2026 that monthly information wasn’t submitted — a weekly MIS fixes both. What I’d own is that MIS: one weekly view from plant to outlet.” Then ask: “Which of these four keeps you up at night?”
