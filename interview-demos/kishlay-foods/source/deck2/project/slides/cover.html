OPENING (30–40 s). “This second deck is the operating view — no marketing. Kishlay is, at heart, a machine that converts commodities — potato, wheat flour, palm oil, corn, gram flour, sugar and laminate — into ₹5 and ₹10 packs, at about 2,600 tonnes a month of capacity across five plants, sold through two lakh outlets in 317 towns. I’ll first reduce the business to five numbers, then walk the machine end to end: the plants, the pack physics, every one of the 236 products and its price point, the distribution network, the competitors category by category, and the four constraints that decide what it earns next.” Use this deck when they want depth on operations; use the first deck for the overall story.
