SKUs 2 OF 4 — EXTRUDED AND FRIED PELLETS (45–60 s). “The second block is the extruded and fried-pellet engine — 73 products: 19 fryms, 7 rings, 8 sticks, 5 balls, 3 puffs, 4 popcorn, 16 ‘other snacks’ and 11 ‘higher-MRP’ snacks. Almost all are ₹5; the Higher-MRP range at ₹10–20 repeats names like Snacky, Sizzling Noodles and Soya Katori in bigger packs.” First principles: one extruder or pellet fryer can make katori, pipe, poochka, pasta and rings just by changing a die and seasoning, so a new SKU costs almost nothing in the plant — the cost shows up later as more stock at the distributor and a longer hanging strip at the kirana. That’s why this block, with the most look-alike items (Onion Ringo vs Onion Rin-Goo, Skyzone vs Sky Zone, Snacky in two categories), is where I’d run an SKU-velocity × margin cut first. Note: the website lists Cheese Balls three times, once misspelt “Chesse”.
