THE RANGE (30–40 s). “Here is every product on the website as one picture — box size is product count. 236 products in 18 categories; 184 of them, nearly four in five, are savoury snacks: chips and snacks 66, namkeen 39, fryms 19 and so on. Bakery is 22, meals and pantry 25, drinks 5. And 236 is only what’s listed: the company says 292-plus SKUs, and its supply-chain hiring talks about 900-plus once you count every pack size. Every name is in the appendix if you want to go through them.” Insight: the long tail on the right — eight categories with three to eight products each — is where a velocity check would start.
