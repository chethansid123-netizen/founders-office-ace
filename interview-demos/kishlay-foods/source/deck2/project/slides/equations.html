FIVE NUMBERS (45–60 s). Point at each tile, one sentence each. “Value per truck: snacks are mostly air, a truck fills on volume, so Kishlay makes at three sites inside the markets it sells to. Unit margin: from a ₹10 pack, about ₹7.22 reaches Kishlay and about 25 paise is left, by my estimate. Capacity: about 2,600 tonnes a month, roughly 50 to 75% used. Reach: about ₹17k of sales per outlet a year, against roughly ₹29k at Gopal. Working capital: 236 products on the site, 292-plus SKUs claimed, 900-plus counting every pack size — all of which has to be stocked and financed. Every slide that follows moves one of these five numbers.” Working: ₹10 − 5% GST (0.48) − retailer ~1.60 − trade ~0.70 = ₹7.22; × 3.5% EBITDA (CRISIL 2.5–4%) ≈ ₹0.25. ₹344 Cr ÷ 2 lakh outlets ≈ ₹17.2k; Gopal ₹1,468 Cr ÷ ~5 lakh ≈ ₹29k. Utilisation: ₹344 Cr ÷ ₹150–220/kg = 15,600–22,900 MT vs 31,200 MT capacity.
