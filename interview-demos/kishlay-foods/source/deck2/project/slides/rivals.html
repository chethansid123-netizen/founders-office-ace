COMPETITION (45 s). “On the left, the economics: Gopal is four times Kishlay’s revenue, Bikaji seven times, and they keep two to three times more of every ₹10 pack. On the right, who Kishlay fights in each aisle: Lay’s, Bingo! and Balaji in chips; Kurkure, Yellow Diamond and Crax in rings; Haldiram’s, Bikaji and Gopal in namkeen; Wai Wai, Maggi and YiPPee! in noodles — Wai Wai is the one that dominates the Northeast; Britannia, Parle and Bisk Farm in biscuits; and the East-India regionals Pran, Annapurna Swadisht and Anmol, which BDO names alongside Kishlay as the region’s snack leaders. So the gap isn’t the factory; it’s pull, mix and sales per outlet.” Kishlay FY24 revenue ₹344 Cr; EBITDA range is CRISIL’s 2.5–4%; Gopal FY25 ₹1,468 Cr, 7.2%; Bikaji FY25 ₹2,562 Cr, 10.5% excluding PLI.
