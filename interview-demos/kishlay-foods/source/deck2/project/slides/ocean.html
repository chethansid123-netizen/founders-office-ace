RED OCEAN, BLUE OCEAN (60 s). “A strategy canvas compares how three types of player compete on eight factors. The nationals — grey dashes — win on ad spend, national reach and premium family packs. Loose local namkeen — yellow — wins only on price and freshness. Kishlay — red — matches the cheapest on price and beats both on things nobody else combines: made locally, regional flavours, kids’ fun with toys in the pack, and range breadth. At ₹5 the water is red: 130-plus rivals, coin prices, rising inputs. The blue pocket is where Kishlay’s red line is high and everyone else is low — regional flavour and local making — pushed up the price ladder. Using the ERRC grid: eliminate look-alike SKUs like Onion Ringo versus Onion Rin-Goo; reduce new ₹5 shapes and me-too biscuits that fight Britannia and Parle head-on; raise Jolokia, Jhalmuri and 40 Aana in 200–400 g packs at ₹55–110; create an NE-flavour range for Bhutan, Nepal and quick commerce.” Method: Kim and Mauborgne’s Blue Ocean Strategy (strategy canvas, ERRC grid); scores are your analyst view.
