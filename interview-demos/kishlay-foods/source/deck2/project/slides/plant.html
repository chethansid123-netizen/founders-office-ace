INSIDE THE HUB (60 s). “Inside the Guwahati hub there are four production units. Potato chips run on stainless-steel PLC/HMI lines from Siddhivinayak Agri Processing, 200 to 1,500 kg an hour — that’s Zing, Flames and Crinkle. Extruded snacks run on Fabcon continuous cooking extruders — rings, Kurkure-type sticks and pasta shapes. Then a balls, tricone and puffs unit, and a biscuits unit for Mamooz and cookies. Two shared systems matter for cost and hygiene: flexible screw conveyors that move flour and spice in a sealed system, and Pace and Astro high-speed packing machines. Quality is a six-step spine: raw material inspection, in-process monitoring, lab testing, packaging inspection, sensory evaluation and a final pre-dispatch check, under ISO 22000:2018, HACCP and FSSAI.” First-principles point: one extruder makes many shapes, so variety is cheap to add on the line but expensive downstream in SKUs and stock. People point: a January 2026 study of a 152-person KFPL unit found 75.65% blue-collar staff and absenteeism peaking in July — which collides with monsoon, the peak snacking season.
