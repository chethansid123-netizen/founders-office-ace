INSIDE THE HUB (45 s). “Four production lines under one roof: potato chips on stainless PLC lines doing up to 1,500 kg an hour; Fabcon extruders for rings, sticks and pasta shapes; a balls and puffs line; and biscuits for Mamooz and cookies. Every pack passes six checks — raw material, in-process, lab, packaging, sensory and pre-dispatch — under ISO 22000, FSSAI and HACCP. The people number to know: Tulsi has 512 people, three in four blue-collar, and a study of one unit found absence peaks in July — exactly when monsoon snacking peaks.” Detail if asked: chips lines from Siddhivinayak Agri Processing, 200–1,500 kg/hr; flexible screw conveyors move flour and spice sealed; Pace and Astro high-speed packers.
