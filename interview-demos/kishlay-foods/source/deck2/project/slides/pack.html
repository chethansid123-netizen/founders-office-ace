PACK PHYSICS (40 s). “Six pack formats, each solving a physical problem: nitrogen-flushed pouches keep ₹5 and ₹10 snacks crisp through the monsoon; a 400 g zipper pouch keeps family namkeen fresh across tea times; cartons, sachets, tetra packs and flow-wrap for the rest. The chart is the point: a muffin lasts about a month, so it has to be sold within a few beats of the plant; namkeen lasts six to nine months and cheese bhujia and tea a year, so those are what travel to far states and Bhutan.” Shelf life by category: eggless muffins ~1 month, rasgulla 1–2, cookies and Gulab Jamun 3–4, Crispy Rusk Toast 5, namkeen, noodles, moong dal 6–9, cheese bhujia and Assam tea 12.
