BigBasket also sells Bikaneri Bhujia 400 g, and Masala Chira appears in trade listings, though neither is on the website. Noodles: 6 products — Zing, Zing Chicken Masala for the Northeast’s non-vegetarian buyers, Sizzling, Miney — mostly ₹5 and ₹10, made on a dedicated plant since 2023, competing head-on with Wai Wai. Soya: Nutri Zing in pouches and a 180 g carton, 52 g protein per 100 g.” First principles: namkeen is where the margin mix improves, because a 400 g pouch at ₹110 carries the same trade and freight cost per trip as a handful of ₹5 sachets but far more rupees. It’s the natural engine for growing sales per outlet.
