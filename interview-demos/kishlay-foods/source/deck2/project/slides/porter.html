FIVE FORCES (45–60 s). “Before asking where to go, ask how hard this arena is. Rivalry in the centre: 4.5 out of 5 — Tracxn tracks 130-plus competitors; the nationals out-spend Kishlay and the regionals out-price it. Suppliers: 4 out of 5 — commodities are 70–75% of cost, and in FY25 palm oil rose about 54% and potato about 56%, as Gopal reported. Buyers: 4 — the shopper pays in coins so MRP can’t rise, and distributors demand margin and credit. Substitutes: 3.5 — street jhalmuri, samosa and loose namkeen are always next door. New entrants: 3 — anyone can buy a fryer and a pouch machine, but nobody quickly builds 2,600 tonnes a month and two lakh outlets. Four of five forces are high: this is a red ocean. So the question is where the blue water is.” The scores are an analyst view; the drivers behind them are sourced.
