DISTRIBUTION (45 s). “Five hops: five plants; C&F agents and super-stockists who buy in container loads on credit; distributors running town beats with vans; wholesale markets like Fancy Bazar in 317 towns; and two lakh-plus kiranas, bakeries, tea stalls and kiosks. Four numbers: about 630 outlets per town; about ₹17k of sales per outlet a year against roughly ₹29k at Gopal; of every ₹10 pack about ₹7.22 reaches Kishlay after GST, the retailer’s margin and the trade’s cut; and a field force of VP Sales, an RSM for the North East, ASMs including a noodles ASM in Tripura, and sales officers in Guwahati, Silchar, Dibrugarh, Tezpur, Nagaon and Tinsukia.” Beyond general trade: BigBasket, B2B marketplaces, exports to Bhutan, and co-packing for Frito-Lay and Britannia.
