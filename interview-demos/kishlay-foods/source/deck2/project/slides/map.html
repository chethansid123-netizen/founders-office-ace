POSITIONING MAP (45–60 s). “Split the market two ways — price focus left to right, regional to national top to bottom — and three things jump out. One, the top-left is crowded: Kishlay, Pran, Annapurna Swadisht, Wai Wai, Bisk Farm, Balaji, Gopal and loose local namkeen all fight for the ₹5 hanging strip. Two, the nationals sit lower: PepsiCo, ITC, Britannia, Parle, Haldiram’s and Bikaji win on reach and ad money, and Haldiram’s and Bikaji own the premium end. Three, the top-right — regional and premium — is nearly empty. Kishlay’s range already stretches to ₹250, and it owns flavours no national makes, like Jolokia Chanachur with Assam’s ghost pepper, Jhalmuri and 40 Aana. That corner is where the next slides point.” Be clear the positions are your analyst view from price lists and reach, not survey data.
