BCG, DIRECTIONAL (45–60 s). Say the caveat first: “Kishlay’s share by category isn’t public, so this is directional — growth from industry reports, strength judged against the category leader in Kishlay’s core states — but the shape is useful.” Then: “The cash cow is the ₹5 extruded and fryms engine — about 120 products, and the company calls Non Stop the number-one extruded snacks player in the Northeast. Protect it and use its cash. The star is namkeen: organised namkeen is the fastest-growing part of the category, peers like Gopal and Bikaji are built on it, and Kishlay has flavours no national makes — invest, especially in 200–400 g packs. The question marks are potato chips, where Lay’s leads, and instant noodles, where Wai Wai dominates the Northeast — decide whether to win the ₹5 Northeast niche or use the lines for contract manufacturing. The dogs are biscuits and cakes against Britannia and Parle, and pantry and drinks — prune or co-pack.” Product counts: namkeen 39; potato-based 17 (6 potato chips + 11 potato items in chips & snacks); extruded and fryms ~120; biscuits and cakes 22 online plus Mamooz offline; pantry and drinks 24 (pickles 10, cereals 6, juices 5, soya 3).
