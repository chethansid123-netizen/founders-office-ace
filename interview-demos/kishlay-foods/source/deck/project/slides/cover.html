THE HOOK (45 seconds). Open with the number, not with yourself. “Same ten-rupee packet. By my estimate from CRISIL’s public margin range, Kishlay keeps about 25 paise of it as operating profit. Gopal keeps about 52. Bikaji about 76. The factory isn’t the difference — all three can make a good namkeen. The difference is pull: brand, mix, and how much each shop sells. Kishlay has already won the hard part in the Northeast — it’s on two lakh shelves. The next decade is about turning that availability into preference, so shoppers ask for Non Stop by name.” Method: assume about ₹7.20 reaches the maker from a ₹10 MRP after 5% GST and trade margins, then apply each company’s EBITDA margin (Kishlay 2.5–4% per CRISIL; Gopal 7.2% FY25; Bikaji 10.5% FY25 excluding PLI). Say “estimate” out loud and invite correction: “you’ll know the real number — I’d love to know how close this is.”
