THE PORTFOLIO (60 s). “What did they build to solve that need? A pack for every coin and every hour of the day. The ladder: ₹5 is trial and kids — rings with toys inside, fryums, even a ₹5 eggless muffin and a ₹5 tea sachet. ₹10 is the habit — Zing chips, peanuts, noodles. ₹20–55 is the household — Potato Feast, butter cookies, rusk, 200 g namkeen. ₹110–250 is the occasion — 400 g zipper bhujia, boxed Gulab Jamun and Rasgulla.” Then the chart: “236 products on the website across 18 categories — 78% savoury. The About page says 292+ SKUs, and supply-chain job posts say 900+ once you count every pack size. That breadth is a weapon with distributors — one supplier fills 18 slots — but it’s also the working-capital problem I’ll come back to.” Brands: Non Stop is the consumer hero (#1 snacks brand in Assam per the company); Mamooz is biscuits; Kishlay is the corporate trust mark plus tea and sweets; Bisk Baker is rusk. Sub-brands: Zing, Nutri Zing, Krunch, Krispy, Twister, Snacky, Miney, Pop Shup. Occasions: morning chai (rusk, Marie), school tiffin (₹5 rings), evening chai (bhujia), night chakna (peanuts, Chakna Mix), quick meal (noodles, soya chunks), festivals (sweets).
