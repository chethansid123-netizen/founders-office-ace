08 / 09

BRAND + WEBSITE UX (75 s). “On Keller’s pyramid, Kishlay is strong at the base: salience — two lakh outlets in its core states. Performance is fine: value and freshness. But judgement — ‘is this a quality brand I trust?’ — is unproven, and resonance — people asking for it by name — is missing. You can see it in brand architecture: four master brands and ten-plus sub-brands, the website says Kishlay while every social handle says Non Stop by Kishlay.” Then the audit, delivered as help, not criticism: “I went through the site like a shopper and like a distributor. Eight things: the Non Stop logo in the homepage hero is mirrored; all 236 products say ‘Contact for Price’, yet there’s a price-range filter; three products called ‘Flames’ and three ‘Butter Cookies’ with no size or MRP to tell them apart; ‘Chesse Balls’ on the site and ‘Supper Hot Balls’ printed on the actual pack; the hero says since 2003 while the Google description says since 1995; all seven milestone photos on the About page return 404; on mobile the homepage is 4.6 MB and 183 requests, and the first screen is just an image; and there’s no way to buy — no Blinkit or BigBasket link, no store finder. Also: ‘200 million customers’ and ‘50 premium products’ on About don’t match the 236 listed. The fix is two front doors: a Non Stop consumer site where you see MRP and size and tap to buy, and a distributor portal with pincode territories, margins and an application form.” Every one of these is a one-week fix — offer to own it.
