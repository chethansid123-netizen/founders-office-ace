THE 90-DAY ROADMAP (75 s). “Here are the 90 days on one page: five workstreams, each with an owner, and three gates. Quick commerce and modern trade: hero SKUs in weeks one and two, onboarding on Blinkit, Zepto and Instamart by week four, then an eight-week pilot with a ten-store modern-trade test, and a decision in week 13. Exports: the Bhutan baseline first; Nepal broker quotes and buyer meetings by week six; two trucks in weeks seven to ten; then a paper on licensing a local maker. In parallel, UAE registration for eight SKUs and samples to Lulu, Al Adil and Nesto, with the shipping-route go or no-go at the end. 3P and M&A: a capacity audit by line in the first three weeks, co-pack renewals and two new requests for quotes, and twenty brands screened down to five. Reporting: an MIS in week two, CRISIL monthly filings resumed and Tulsi’s GST filing backlog cleared by week four, a board pack and first update by week six, and the FY27–29 plan at the end. And under all of it, the operating rhythm: a twice-weekly review of the tracker, a decision log, and a 48-hour escalation rule. Three gates: week four, week eight, week 13. Total budget about ₹57 lakh, of which ₹35 lakh is stock that sells — so the real cash at risk is about ₹22 lakh.” Engagement: “Which of these would you want to see first — I’d suggest the MIS, because every gate depends on it.” Transition: “Which brings me to how we’d run it.”
