THE OPERATING SYSTEM (60 s). “A plan only works if everyone looks at the same numbers. So one monthly page with fifteen KPIs, each with a target and an owner. A few to call out: revenue around ₹28 Cr a month in FY27; contribution per kilo by channel and utilisation by line — the two that tell us whether the plan is working; bank-limit use back under 80%, from 93% in 2023; cash accrual of at least ₹15 Cr a year, which is what CRISIL expected; and CRISIL and GST filings on time every month — Tulsi had five GST filing delays in FY26. The rhythm: a daily sell-out dashboard for the new channels, a twice-weekly review of the 26 tasks in the tracker, a 30-minute founders’ review on Mondays, and the board, investor and lender pack by the fifth working day. Anything blocked for 48 hours goes to the founders. And credibility comes first: resume the monthly filings with CRISIL, share the FY25 and FY26 accounts and clear the GST backlog by week four, so the ‘Issuer Not Cooperating’ tag can come off — that matters to lenders, to any seller in an acquisition, and to any investor. In week one, I’d ask for four things: SKU-level sales and margin, line-wise output, the scheme ledger and the CRISIL correspondence.” Engagement: “What does the monthly review look like today?” Transition: “So what will be on the table on day 90?”
