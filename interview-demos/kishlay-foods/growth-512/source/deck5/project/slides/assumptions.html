ASSUMPTIONS (use if asked). “These are the thirteen assumptions that move the answer, each with a bear, base and bull value and a test. The two that matter most are FY26 growth after FY25’s fall — I assume 8% — and the starting EBITDA margin of about 6%; both can be replaced with your numbers in week one. The channel assumptions — dark stores, rate of sale, modern-trade stores — are tested by the Guwahati pilots. The export ones by a customs broker’s quote, the first two trucks and the UAE registrations. Switch the scenario cell in the model and every sheet updates: bear ₹338 Cr at 6.8%, base ₹458 Cr at 10%, bull ₹564 Cr at 12.7%.”
