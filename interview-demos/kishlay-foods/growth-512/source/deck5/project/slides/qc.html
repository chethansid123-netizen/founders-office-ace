ENGINE 1A: QUICK COMMERCE (75 s). “First engine. Non Stop is already priced right against the leaders: Aloo Bhujia 200 grams at ₹55, the same as Haldiram’s; Bikaneri Bhujia 400 grams at ₹110 against Bikaji’s ₹110; ₹5 and ₹10 packs at the same price as Crax, Yellow Diamond, Wai Wai and Lay’s. Those prices come from BigBasket and indexed listings. But when I searched indexed Blinkit and Zepto listings, Non Stop didn’t show up. I’ll confirm that live tonight — the Price_Audit sheet has the ten packs and four apps to screenshot. The prize: quick commerce is about ₹64,000 Cr of sales in FY25 heading to ₹2 lakh Cr by FY28 per CareEdge, and Blinkit has served Guwahati since January 2025. If Kishlay gets into 1,000 dark stores across the East and Northeast with six SKUs selling one unit a day at ₹62 net, that’s ₹13.6 Cr a year by FY29, ramping from ₹3.4 Cr in FY27. Modern trade adds ₹18.8 Cr — I’ll show how we break in on the next slide. The pilot is eight weeks in Guwahati for about ₹9 lakh, including ₹4 lakh of stock. Packs to test: a party box of twelve ₹10 packs, a chai-time duo, and a Northeast heat pack with Jolokia and Jhalmuri — these are ideas to cost with finance. Gates: live on all three apps by week four, at least one unit per SKU per store per day, 25% repeat in 30 days, and 15% contribution after platform fees and ads. Pass, and Kolkata and Siliguri follow. Fail, and we stop — the stock sells through general trade, so the real money at risk is about ₹5 lakh.” Engagement: “Has the team tried quick commerce before? What happened?” Transition: “Quick commerce is one half of the first engine; modern trade is the other.”
