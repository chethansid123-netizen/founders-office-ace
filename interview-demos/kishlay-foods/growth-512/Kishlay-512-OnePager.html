Is ₹512 Cr right?

No public source states it. From FY25’s ₹288 Cr it needs 15.5% a year to FY29 (about 2× the category) and +78% by FY27 (fails). Base case: ₹458 Cr and ₹46 Cr EBITDA (10%) in FY29; ₹512 Cr arrives in FY31, or in FY29 only on the bull case (₹564 Cr). Bear ₹338 Cr. Commit to ~₹460 Cr and ~₹46 Cr EBITDA; keep ₹512 Cr as the stretch; steer by EBITDA (listed peers trade at ~9× EBITDA, ~1× revenue). Precedent: Annapurna Swadisht, a Kolkata ₹5-pack peer, reached ₹520 Cr at ~11.6% EBITDA in FY26.
